2026-11-01 impact feedback:
Three bounded GPUParticles3D burst systems add sea spray, hull splinters,
and shore dust. Each has 16 reusable burst slots: 384 / 192 / 128 particles
capacity respectively. Each active system shares a simulation and draw;
there is no allocation per impact. Sea impacts retain water ripples. Land
impacts now emit dust rather than an erroneous water splash. Projectile
trajectory, hit detection, damage, lifetime and gameplay RNG are unchanged.
Existing CC0 smoke flipbook supplies the dust; spray/splinters use the
engine's default particle texture with velocity stretching.

Combat particle nodes are recreated at match reset only, clearing previous
smoke and burst slots; ordinary combat reuses them. Volcanic ash is separate.
