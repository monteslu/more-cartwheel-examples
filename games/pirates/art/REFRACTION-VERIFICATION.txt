Pirates refraction verification — 2026-10-31

Symptom: switching maps produced bright jagged foam around floating cargo,
even though CargoGroup and all ship roots were excluded from refraction.
Recursive visibility hiding did not fix it; that experiment was removed.

Cause: three.c auto-instance vertex buffers were overwritten while an
external command encoder still referenced their previous contents.
set_external_encoder(NULL) flushes/detaches and advances the serial, but
cannot submit the caller-owned encoder. The next render reused the buffer.
Rebuilding Environment after CargoGroup changed batch ordering, making
refraction geometry use transforms belonging to the main view's cargo.

Fix lives in cartwheel-engine/deps/three.c/src/gen_wgpu.c. Both dynamic
attribute and float-stream writes rename their buffer while stream_ext says
external commands remain outstanding, matching the uniform stream lifetime.
This is a local engine dependency change and must accompany Pirates when
integrated; it is not present in the repository's previously pinned SDK.

Automated regression: test/test_wgpu_external.c case O renders a shared
geometry batch into two targets on one external encoder, detaching between
renders and moving the batch before the second render. The first target
must still match an independently submitted reference image.
- Updated renderer: full test_wgpu_external passed; no renderer errors or
  WebGPU validation errors.
- Negative control: rebuilt original HEAD gen_wgpu.c into a separate archive
  under /tmp/pirates-buffer-control. Same test fails specifically at
  'detached external encoder retains its original instance transforms'.
  The changed-image control passes in both versions.
