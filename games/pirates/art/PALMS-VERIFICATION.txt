Original coconut palms, 2026-11-01

tools/make-palms.py creates the editable art/coconut-palm.blend and packed
assets/models/coconut-palm.glb. Replaces the packed jetski palm asset with
a curved, ringed trunk and eight arching fronds with individual leaflets.
Leaf front/back surfaces are separated slightly to avoid coplanar artifacts.
Leaflet width is exaggerated enough to remain legible at the gameplay camera.
Original art is MIT; credits and pack roots updated. Existing palm positions,
height scaling, harbor exclusions and gameplay logic remain unchanged.

Built pirates and pirates_auto. Loaded final playable cart through romdev;
inspected solo Lagoon and four-human volcanic Race at 1920x1080. Both returned
render.skipped=0. Evidence:
out/pirates-lagoon-palms.png
out/pirates-volcanic-palms.png

The sampled Lagoon scene reports 405,066 triangles and 49 draw calls versus
368,202 triangles and 48 calls with the prior palms. The final width/color
adjustments preserve topology. This is a deliberate art-detail cost, not a
performance improvement or proof of sustained frame rate. GPU timing remains
unavailable. Initial thin/coplanar leaf surfaces were revised after screenshot
inspection; only the corrected model is packed.
