Recovery and pause verification — 2026-10-31

Captains receive 240 simulation ticks (four seconds) of protection only
when respawning after sinking. Starting a fresh match grants none. Human
and AI captains share the rule. Cannon and environmental damage are ignored
while protected; firing immediately removes protection. A countdown label
above the ship and PROTECTED status in its card explain the state.

ESC / START pauses an active match. Any active human captain can pause or
resume. Q / SELECT while paused returns to the title screen to choose a
new match. Input is handled in the app update callback, which remains live
while Cartwheel's scene tree is paused. A state-transition guard prevents
the START press that begins a match from immediately pausing it.
Reset commands explicitly unpause the scene.

Engine change: src/water/water.c must accompany this game. Its render clock
previously interpolated with platform alpha even while tree ticks were
frozen, making paused water shift slightly. Paused rendering now uses the
frozen tree tick. Before this fix, screenshots 120 frames apart had 8078
pixels differing by more than two channel levels. After the fix the entire
1920x1080 screenshots match exactly (zero differing pixels):
