Coast smoothing, 2026-11-01

Updated tools/make-coast.py and regenerated both maps with Blender 4.2.9.
The existing rounded collision outlines remain unchanged. Beach cells within
2 meters of the shoreline now share quarter-meter edge samples and center
samples, evaluated against the terrain function. Transition fans include the
same edge vertices, avoiding cracks against the coarser inland/seabed mesh.
Ridge falloff changed from a fractional-power cusp to a zero-slope shoulder.
Generated height headers and baked terrain textures were refreshed as well.
Prop heights still interpolate the half-meter heightfield; the added shoreline
samples evaluate the same surface directly rather than that approximation.

Exported terrain counts: coast 61,442 triangles; lagoon 62,798 triangles.
Built pirates and pirates_auto successfully in build/pirates-current-cart.
Loaded the rebuilt pirates.wasc with romdev and ran solo Voyage on both maps.
Inspected full-resolution 1920x1080 screenshots. Softer hill shoulders and
finer beach edges retain the bays, headlands and narrow land connections.
render.skipped was 0 on each map. This is visual verification, not an FPS test.

Follow-up: inland corner/crease smoothing, 2026-11-01

Filtered the actual inland mesh heights with a separable five-tap binomial
kernel (half-meter standard deviation), fading in from 0.25 to 1.25 meters
behind the coastline. This reduces pointed ridge intersections and geometric
spikes rather than only changing normals. Refined vertices interpolate the
same height correction; exported prop-height tables contain the new surface.
The coastline polygons, beach waterline, connectivity and mesh counts remain
unchanged. Regenerated both Blender scenes, albedos, GLBs and height headers.

Built pirates and pirates_auto. Loaded the rebuilt playable cart through
romdev and inspected 1920x1080 screenshots after 60 rendered frames on each
map. render.skipped was 0 for both. Hilltops and volcano shoulders are softer;
this is a targeted smoothing improvement, not final art-quality approval.
Evidence: out/pirates-corners-before.png, out/pirates-corners-after.png,
out/pirates-lagoon-corners-before.png, out/pirates-lagoon-corners-after.png.

Follow-up: continuous beach-to-inland profile, 2026-11-01

Removed the clamped linear height ramp and clamped relief amplitude from
tools/make-coast.py. Exponential easing now approaches the inland plateau
continuously, removing the derivative discontinuities at 1 and 3.8 meters
inland. Regenerated both maps with Blender, including GLBs, albedo atlases,
Blender sources and prop-height headers. Collision outlines are unchanged.
Built pirates and pirates_auto cart targets successfully. Loaded the new
playable cart with romdev, ran 60 frames of solo Voyage on each map, and
inspected 1920x1080 screenshots. Both reported cw.gpu.errors=0 and
render.skipped=0. This is a targeted terrain improvement; remaining cliff
texture banding and coarse prop geometry are not resolved by this change.

Follow-up: waterline ledges, 2026-11-01

Removed the 0.1-meter height discontinuity at signed coast distance zero.
The inland base now meets the submerged shelf at zero height and slope .85;
noise fades quadratically to preserve that tangent. Broadened the inland
binomial height filter from five to seven taps (sigma approximately .61 m).
Regenerated both Blender scenes, GLBs, albedos and prop-height headers.
Collision polygons and navigation layout are unchanged.

Built both native and cart pirates/pirates_auto targets. Loaded playable
pirates.wasc through romdev, rendered 250 frames on each map, inspected
1920x1080 screenshots: softer shore transitions and ridge shoulders, with
bays and isthmuses retained. Both maps: cw.gpu.errors=0, render.skipped=0.
This is a modest smoothing change, not final realistic terrain approval.
The build also contains the previously generated harbor texture atlases.

out/pirates-corners-lagoon-before.png, out/pirates-corners-lagoon-after.png.
