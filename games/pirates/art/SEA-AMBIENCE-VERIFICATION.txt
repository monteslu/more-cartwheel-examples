Wind/surf ambience, 2026-11-01

tools/make-ambience.py creates original stereo filtered-noise surf and shared
low-frequency wind, with offset swell envelopes and a half-second loop blend.
Standard-library Python only; no external recordings or downloads. Asset:
assets/audio/sea.wav, 12 seconds, stereo PCM16, 24000 Hz. Packed in roots.txt.
Mixer starts one looping sample voice at volume .23, two-second fade-in,
max_voices=1 and priority 1. It persists through menus/pause/match resets,
and is stopped/freed at shutdown. Existing game RNG and SFX are unchanged.

Source analysis in sea-audio-analysis.json: RMS .119996, peak .721863,
zero clipped samples. Left/right loop-boundary deltas .023773/.019379 are
below ordinary adjacent-sample p99 .171722. This is a seam signal check,
not a listening judgment. Runtime gain reduces source levels further.
