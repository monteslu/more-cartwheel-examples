Continuous cart profiling — 2026-11-01

Direct perf reads include the time between the previous frame and the read
command, because the engine records frame intervals at the beginning of a
frame before processing the mailbox. Even stepping 900 then reading perf600
included a 497ms tool gap. Do not classify such maxima as gameplay hitches.

pirates_profile defers its reply until N+2 frames have passed and runs perf N
inside that continuing batch. Default 300, range 1..590; use maxFrames:600.
No gameplay state is modified. This is an opt-in debug command.
