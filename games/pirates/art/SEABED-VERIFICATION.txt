Seamless island shelf, 2026-11-01

The previous mesh had a hard submerged boundary visible as a dark polygon
around each island. Live romdev checks hiding the seabed and disabling its
received shadows isolated the terrain/floor join rather than floor shadows.

The generator now eases the shelf slope into -11.75 meters, matching the top
of the scene's floor box (center -12, thickness 0.5). A quadratic transition
begins at 7.75 meters depth and reaches a horizontal tangent at 11.75 meters.
The baked material smoothly loses its noise and blends to the floor's exact
sand color between depths 8 and 11.5. Flat redundant cells are omitted.
Both Blender scenes, GLBs, textures and surface-height headers regenerated.
No changes to water settings, collision outlines, simulation or engine code.
Coast: 70,532 triangles; Lagoon: 71,992 (previously 61,442 and 62,798).

Built native and cart pirates / pirates_auto. Loaded playable cart through
romdev, ran solo Voyage for 250 rendered frames on each map, inspected
1920x1080 screenshots. Angular dark shelf outline is absent in both new
screenshots. cw.gpu.errors=0 and render.skipped=0 on both maps.
