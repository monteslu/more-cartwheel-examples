Shore refraction correction, 2026-11-01

Dark blue slivers appeared between beach geometry and foam at some wave
phases. Setting Water.distortion=0 in romdev removed the reproduced defect.
The water shader previously applied the same screen-space refraction offset
at the shoreline and offshore, allowing it to sample terrain clipped out of
the refraction target.

Engine src/water/extensions/water.mjs now uses the existing undistorted
baked floor depth to fade refraction displacement smoothly from zero at
0.5 m to full strength at 3 m. Outside the floor map / without a floor map,
the existing depth fallback is 100 m, retaining full distortion. Reflection
displacement is unchanged. No added texture sample: the floor lookup is
moved earlier and reused. Physical waves, foam, buoyancy and gameplay remain
unchanged. Default scene distortion is still 0.018, verified in romdev.

Regenerated Pirates GL and WebGPU program tables explicitly with --table
(the missing-state survey alone correctly reported no missing states and
would not regenerate changed shader code). Tint accepted all 15 programs.
Both native and cart pirates / pirates_auto rebuilt successfully.

Romdev screenshots inspected at 1920x1080:

The reproduced slivers are absent in the inspected new screenshots. Both
maps report GPU errors 0 and render.skipped 0. This is sampled visual
verification, not proof for every possible view or arbitrary engine scene.

Native 1500-frame replay gates pass on both GL and WebGPU, matching the
hull-clearance expected state, hash 726034506. Outputs:
 out/pirates-shore-gl/ (10.5 seconds)
 out/pirates-shore-webgpu/ (10.3 seconds)
No separate performance claim is made from those wall times.

Bundled patch engine-patches/water-shore-refraction.patch and extended
prepare-engine.py to install/check all three fixes. Verified on temporary
shared clones at the documented pins: read-only check rejects missing fixes,
--apply installs all three, repeated check accepts them, resulting water
shader bytes match the working engine exactly. Existing unrelated edits in
the development engine were not included in the new patch.
