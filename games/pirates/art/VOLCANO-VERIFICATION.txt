Volcanic rockfall gameplay — 2026-10-31

The Gilded Shoals now has three marked rockfall zones per eruption. First
warning starts at simulation tick 1080 (18 seconds), then every 1800 ticks.
The warning lasts 210 ticks (3.5 seconds); the final 90 ticks show rotating
rock models arcing from the crater. Each impact damages ships within 3.2 m
by 28 hull and creates a water splash. Six fixed water locations alternate
between volleys. Target centers remain more than 9.2 m from every harbor,
keeping the six-metre docking area outside the damage radius. AI captains,
merchant and patrol steer away when within six metres of a warning.
Palm Lagoon has no volcanic hazard. Match restart clears warning/counters.
A 48-particle ash plume identifies the volcano. Three reusable rock nodes
bound the hazard geometry. Warnings use projected world-space dashed rings
and a countdown. State hashing includes the map and eruption state.
