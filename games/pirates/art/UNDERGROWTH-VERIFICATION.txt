Broadleaf understory, 2026-11-01

Replaced the packed jetski shrub with an original Blender-authored coastal
broadleaf model (tools/make-undergrowth.py, art/undergrowth.blend,
assets/models/undergrowth.glb). Curved leaves have a raised midrib, thin solid
geometry and smooth normals. Three rough materials cover leaves and branches.
Placement leaves irregular clearings and keeps these plants below the palms.
Ground attachment uses the terrain height rather than the old shrub offset.
Credits and roots.txt updated. The old shrub file is no longer packed.

Built pirates and pirates_auto. Loaded the final playable cart in romdev;
inspected 1920x1080 screenshots of solo Lagoon and four-human volcanic Race.
Both returned render.skipped=0. Evidence:
out/pirates-lagoon-undergrowth-final.png
out/pirates-volcanic-undergrowth-final.png

The first leaf mesh raised Lagoon's reported triangle count to 408,270.
Reducing authored leaf segments from eight to five retained clean leaf shapes
and lowered the final count to 368,202 (48 draws, 11 programs). The old shrub
scene was 337,782 triangles and 46 draws, so this is still an art-cost increase.
These are runtime scene counters, not just source asset counts. No FPS claim:
the short stepped samples include host/tool scheduling, and GPU timers are
unavailable. A decimated experiment introduced visibly ragged leaves and was
discarded. The final exported model uses the original structured topology.

Further visual polish and controlled performance testing remain necessary.
