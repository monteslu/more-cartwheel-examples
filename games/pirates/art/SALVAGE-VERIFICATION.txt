Salvage and restart rules, 2026-10-31

Sinking now drops the actual held resource types into a separate pool of
30 salvage slots. The 18 regular map spawns remain independent. Salvage
expires after 30 seconds and does not become a permanent resource spawn.
Drops snap to navigable water. Banked score, stored supplies and upgrades
survive sinking; a new match clears them and hides the extra sail.
Regular cargo returns to its original position and type on respawn/reset.
