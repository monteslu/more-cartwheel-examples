Crater lava detail, 2026-11-01

Rebuilt original make-lava.py asset. Replaced straight Voronoi plate edges
with shared, densely sampled warped boundaries; 24 unevenly spaced sites
with minimum spacing avoid tiny sliver plates. Variable shrink leaves
uneven fissures. Raised crust interiors and bevel-like lips add thickness.
Smooth crust normals avoid the first revision's faceted appearance.

Baked world-space noise into 512x512 albedo and emission atlases for basalt
grain and spatial heat variation. Embedded both textures in the GLB, with
one material instead of two. Asset is 2,134 triangles and 299,340 bytes;
previous asset was 185 triangles. This is more geometry, not a free effect,
but reduces this asset's material draws from two to one. No per-frame
procedural evaluation or extra lights. No gameplay/collision changes.
Original source and baked images are in art/crater-lava.blend,
art/lava-albedo.png and art/lava-emission.png.

Regenerated GL/WebGPU program tables: 16 states; Tint accepted all, including
new standard+map+rep+emap+senv+ds+l2000. Built native and cart targets.
Loaded playable cart through romdev, ran solo Voyage 250 rendered frames.

camera (25,24,-2.9), FOV 20. Restored (0,64,23), FOV 46 afterward. Final
