Terrain material relief, 2026-11-01

Added baked 2048x2048 tangent normal maps to both terrain assets. The Blender
generator combines world-space noise with lightly weighted, domain-warped
fissures. A vertex authoring mask concentrates relief on exposed limestone
and basalt, keeps grass subtle, and fades to zero at the beach/waterline.
The initial stronger, regular Voronoi pattern looked like paving stones in
romdev; revised before acceptance with weaker/distorted fissures.

Geometry, terrain heights and collision polygons are unchanged. Counts:
Coast 70,532 triangles; Lagoon 71,992 triangles. Both GLBs now have base-color
and normal textures. Baked authoring vertex-color streams are excluded from
export; runtime attributes are POSITION/NORMAL/TEXCOORD_0. Final asset sizes
3,558,008 and 3,552,684 bytes. Each normal atlas adds texture residency and a
material texture sample; unchanged triangles do not imply zero GPU cost.
Editable blend files retain procedural nodes and packed albedo/normal maps.

Generated native GL and WebGPU shader tables, including
standard+map+rep+nmap+shadow+senv+ds+l2000+s100. Final tables contain 17 states;
Tint validation passed (the capture cache also retains a discarded vertex-
color normal-map variant). Both pirates/pirates_auto native and carts built.

Loaded the playable cart in romdev and rendered 250 frames on each map.
Inspected 1920x1080 normal-camera captures; final rock relief is visible on
volcano/cliffs, subtle on Lagoon's low grassy terrain. Each map reported
cw.gpu.errors=0 and render.skipped=0. The Lagoon step request was slow but
completed on its original handle; no FPS claim is made from that delay.
Evidence: out/pirates-surface-{coast,lagoon}-{before,after}.png.

Native WebGPU 1500-frame replay passed all unchanged expectations (13.7 s
wall time): out/pirates-surface-webgpu. This is material detail improvement,
not final art-quality approval or a full performance benchmark.
Native GL 1500-frame replay also passed (12.8 s wall time):
out/pirates-surface-gl. No gameplay baseline changes were needed.
