SKY FIGHTER — CONTROLS AND AUDIO AUDIT

Scope: keyboard and positional gamepad input in the real wasmcart runtime,
weapon readiness, simultaneous actions, menu/state transitions and audio.
Single player only: controller port 0; no co-pilot or weapon-selection mode.

FIXES
- Guided missile taps are buffered for 1.25 seconds through lock acquisition
  or reload. Holding West/X or M waits for readiness and repeats at the
  existing 65-tick reload interval. Releasing stops repetition. Pausing,
  retrying or leaving play cancels pending fire. No unguided ammo is spent.
- The HUD distinguishes acquisition/queued, guided-ready, reload and empty
  ammo; queued, expired and successful launches have explicit feedback.
- RT guns and LT flares now actually trigger at the declared 0.35 threshold;
  their action deadzone previously raised the effective threshold to 0.50.
- An exhausted burner remains off until the button is released and fuel
  reaches 5%. This eliminates the prior near-empty relight/cut oscillation.
  Airbrake takes priority when brake and afterburner are held together.
- Burner audio uses the continuous recording with smooth, rate-correct
  attack/release; the extra synthetic pulse tone was removed.
- The engine's cart audio ring was only 4096 frames, although one render can
  publish up to 8192. A real 100 ms host step generated 4800 frames, overwriting
  704 of its own unread frames. The canonical cartwheel-engine source now
  provides a 16384-frame ring with maximum-burst and power-of-two assertions.
  Sky now builds directly from this canonical workspace engine source; the
  numbered patch exports retain the history of the promoted fixes.
- The Controls page now includes target cycling (North/Y or I).

BINDINGS AND SEMANTICS
Flight: Left stick (0.06 deadzone), D-pad, arrows or WASD. Pitch inversion
        and Expert controls are saved settings. Opposing directions cancel.
Guns: South/A, RT >=0.35, Space or Return. Hold for continuous fire.
Missile: West/X or M. Tap to queue; hold to acquire/fire/repeat. Guided only.
Target: North/Y or I during play. One cycle per press, not per held frame.
Burner: East/B or either Shift. Hold; release/recharge after exhaustion.
Brake: Left shoulder or left Ctrl. Hold; takes priority over burner.
Flares: right-stick click/R3, LT >=0.35 or F. One salvo per press; seven-second
        recharge. Holding the button does not consume another salvo on refill.
Stunts: Right shoulder + stick, or R + arrows/WASD. Q/E directly roll.
Look: Right stick (0.08 deadzone). Release to restore the chase view.
Pause: Start, P or Escape. Press again to resume; East/B also resumes.
Menus: D-pad/stick or arrows/WASD to move focus; South/A, Space or Return
       to select. West/X or M opens Controls on the mission grid.
Settings: North/Y or I on title/pause/results; East/B or Escape returns.
Mouse: Existing visible mission/settings/pause buttons remain clickable.
Throttle: Cruise/afterburner/airbrake are discrete speed modes; there is no
          separate analog throttle or weapon-mode switching binding.

ACTUAL VALIDATION
- scripts/playtest-controls-audit.mjs: real pad/trigger injection in romdev,
  guided tap before lock, released tap during reload, held repeat, release,
  pending-fire cancellation on pause, target-cycle tap/hold, partial RT/LT,
  simultaneous gun/burner/steering/brake and exhaustion/release/rearm.
- scripts/playtest-keyboard-audit.mjs: installed Chrome, actual CartHostWeb
  and Metal, raw keyDown/up through the ABI: Return, WASD/arrows, Space,
  both Shifts, Ctrl, Q/E, R+arrows, M, F, I, P, Escape and menu/back paths.
  The harness completes spread warmup before testing. An initial premature
  Return during warmup was a test error, not a keyboard regression; no
  keyboard bridge or engine keyboard change was retained.
- scripts/playtest-audio-continuity.mjs: direct ABI-ring extraction captures
  528000 stereo frames at 48000 Hz: exactly 11 seconds, including the loop
  wrap. No silent 50 ms windows; RMS 0.07276–0.08572; music underflows zero.
  audioDebug.record returned only one 17 ms frame despite a longer request,
  so that result was discarded as evidence of long-run continuity.
- cartwheel-engine/tests/cart-audio-ring.mjs: real 17/86/100/170/250 ms host
  steps check burst size, allocation/nonoverlap, power-of-two wrapping,
  maximum burst and ABI conformance; the old-ring control must fail.
- Existing menu/SRAM, combat and flight tests remain regression checks.

Receipts and recorded WAV: build/review/controls-audit/.
Hardware limit: these are actual local runtime tests, not a physical
cabinet controller calibration or a listening test on Batocera's speakers.
Local continuity does not establish that a host/device has no underruns.
