# Horde: the eight-level descent

The campaign is eight arenas, one under the other. Each one ends the same way. The team clears its
waves, the boss falls in the last wave, and every enemy left in the arena melts. A hatch in the middle of
the floor opens, and the level is won when one living player walks into it. Each hatch goes deeper
than the last (lift, sluice, crypt stair, crucible, root pit, altar, rift), each arena is darker and
more gothic than the one above it, and the eighth is a burning core.

It stays a twin-stick shooter: there is no inventory and no stats screen. The descent brings new
enemies to learn, a named demon to beat on every floor, a new gun on every floor, and loot showers
whose colour tells you how good the drop is.

This file is the design (sections 1 to 4), then the asset contract the code reads (section 5).
Everything it names is generated by this game's own tools (section 6).

## 1. Shape of a run

| | |
|---|---|
| Levels | 8. Levels 1 to 4 have 5 waves and levels 5 to 8 have 6, so 44 waves in all. The last wave of a level is its boss wave. |
| Wave length | 25 s (the yard's 30 s gave a 20-wave run; 44 waves at 30 s would be over 30 minutes). The boss wave lasts until the boss falls. |
| Run length | About 26 minutes for the full descent: 44 waves of 25 s, about 1 minute per boss and about 20 s per transition. A level takes 2.5 to 4 minutes, so a session can stop at any hatch. |
| Between levels | The boss dies, the crowd melts (2 s), and a loot shower falls. The hatch opens. When the first player steps in, the screen fades and the team stands up on the next level's closed hatch. Upgrades and score carry over, and power-ups end at the hatch. |
| Checkpoint | The title screen offers "Continue from level N" for the deepest level reached, at the levels a played run would have there (as `HORDE_WAVE` does now). |
| Difficulty | Enemy health and count go up by wave inside a level, as in the yard. Each level starts a step above the previous level's first wave, not at its last: the first wave of a new floor is a breather where the new monster is met in small numbers. |
| Roster | A level keeps the three or four kinds before it and adds its own new basic monster (section 3). Older kinds keep coming in smaller shares, so the crowd is mixed and the new kind is the one to learn. |

Wave plans use these kinds. R runner, B brute, L bloater (the yard's three); the new basic kinds are
named per level. "40 R" means a share of the wave's crowd, not a count: the director keeps its count
rule (more with the wave and with players) and draws kinds by these shares.

## 2. Loot showers and rarity

When a boss dies it bursts into a shower of gems and 3 to 5 loot caches, more with more players. A
cache is a small iron-bound coffer with a coloured glow, and a beam of its colour stands over it until
someone takes it. Opening one gives a power-up weapon from the level's unlocked pool at that rarity.
Brutes and level-specific elites can drop a single cache (Common or Uncommon).

| Rarity | Colour (sRGB) | Power-up time | Damage | Extra |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | white-grey `#d8d8d8` | 12 s | x1.0 | - |
| Uncommon | green `#5cf05c` | 15 s | x1.15 | - |
| Rare | blue `#4d8cff` | 18 s | x1.3 | +1 projectile |
| Epic | violet `#b55cff` | 24 s | x1.5 | +1 projectile, pierces 1 more |
| Legendary | orange-gold `#ff9a26` | 30 s | x1.8 | the gun's own perk (each weapon lists one below) |

Odds from a boss cache: Common 30%, Uncommon 30%, Rare 22%, Epic 13%, Legendary 5%, shifting up one
step every two levels, so level 7 and 8 bosses drop about 15% Legendary. The drop is colour-coded by
the cache glow, its ground ring, its beam and its HUD tag, so the team sees what fell from across the
arena. The colours are the familiar five-step loot ladder; none of them is tied to any one game.

## 3. The levels

Every arena keeps the yard's footprint: 76 m square, the shared camera looking down about 62
degrees from the south, the team spawning on the hatch in the middle, enemies pouring in through gaps
round the edge. Section 5 gives the coordinates; the sketches here are 19 x 19 cells of 4 m, north up.
`#` solid wall or deep scenery, `o` cover (pillar, block), `c` crates or barrels of the level's
kind, `~` hazard strip, `H` the hatch, `g` a gap the horde comes through, `+` power-up spot.

### Level 1. The Yard (dusk)

The arena the game has now: a walled container yard at dusk, a low warm sun, cool floodlights, wet
patches. It teaches the basics.

- **Palette and light.** Warm sodium dusk against blue rim light. Grade `dusk.cube`. The lightmap is
  the existing `yard.cwlm`.
- **Layout.** The cinder ring at 17 m with four gaps, eight pillars at 28 m, crates and barrels. The
  centre is open.
- **Waves.** 1: R. 2: R, B. 3: R, L. 4: R, B, L. 5: R, B, L and the boss.
- **New monster.** The runner, the brute and the bloater as they are.
- **Boss: Kilnmaw, the Yard Tyrant.** This is the existing boss and its pattern: it walks, then
  stands and glows for one second (the tell), charges in a straight line, and calls runners. Phase 2,
  under half health, makes the charges come in pairs.
- **New weapon.** The rifle, plus the power-up pool: shotgun, minigun, rockets, grenades, shield,
  magnet.
- **Hatch: freight lift.** A concrete plinth with hazard-striped steel doors that split and sink. A
  lift cage glows amber below.

  A square channel of knee-deep water (`~`) rings the middle at 16 to 22 m. Wading slows every
  player and every walker by 30%, and skitters swim it at full speed. Four dry causeways cross it:
  the gaps in the north and south rows and the open middle of the east and west sides. They are the
  choke points. Brick vault piers (`o`) are cover. The six edge gaps are open culvert mouths.
- **Waves.** 1: 70 R, 30 skitter (meet them). 2: 50 R, 50 skitter. 3: 40 R, 20 B, 40 skitter.
  4: 30 R, 20 L, 50 skitter (a swarm wave). 5: the boss, 60 skitter, 40 R.
- **New monster: the skitter.** It is a six-legged drain crawler, knee-high and wider than it is
  tall, with a flat shell, a pale belly and two hooked forelegs. It moves fast and low in a
  side-to-side scuttle (1.3x runner speed) and swims the water channel without slowing. Its attack
  is a leap: it crouches for 0.4 s (its forelegs rise, which is the tell), springs 3 m, and bites. It
  has little health (60% of a runner) and comes in packs.
- **Boss: Vhessa, Mother of the Drains.** A crab-backed matriarch, 5 m across, with a ridged shell,
  egg sacs glowing sick green along her flanks, two great pincers and a fan of legs.
  - *Pincer sweep*, the close attack: she raises both pincers for 0.8 s, then sweeps a 140-degree arc
    in front of her.
  - *Acid jet*: she rears up and her belly glows for 1.2 s, then she sprays a 40-degree cone of acid
    that leaves burning green puddles for 6 s.
  - *Brood*: she squats for 1 s and her sacs pulse, then she drops 8 skitters behind her.
  - Phases: above 50% health she sweeps and jets in turn. Below 50% she broods every 12 s and moves
    faster. Below 20% her shell cracks and glows, she takes double damage from behind, and she jets
    twice in a row.
  - Clips: walk (a sideways crab gait), attack (the sweep), special (rear and jet), summon (squat
    and pulse).
- **New weapon: the arc caster.** A stubby coil gun that throws lightning. Each shot jumps between up
  to 4 enemies within 5 m of each other. A target standing in water passes the arc to everything in
  the same water within 6 m. Legendary perk: the chain doesn't end, it keeps jumping while
  targets remain. The railgun joins the pool.
- **Hatch: sluice iris.** A round iron cover in a brick collar. Its six leaves turn and slide into
  the collar, and a rung ladder and stair drop into green-lit water below.

### Level 3. The Ossuary (candlelit, bone)

Below the drains are catacombs: walls of stacked skulls and long bones, low stone vaults on squat
columns, alcoves full of dead candles, and a few still burning.

- **Palette and light.** Bone white and ochre in warm candlelight against cold black-blue shadow.
  There is no sky. The key light is a few iron braziers and candle clusters (warm, low, short
  range, all baked) and a dim blue fill from above. The darkness between the pools is a big part of
  the look. Fog is low and brown. The grade is `ossuary.cube`, warm mids and blue-black shadows.
- **Layout.**

  ```
  # . o . c . . . . . . . . . c . o . #
  # . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . #
  # . + . . o . . . H . . . o . . + . #
  # . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . #
  # . o . c . . . . . . . . . c . o . #
  g . . . . . . . o . o . . . . . . . g
  # # # . # # # . . + . . # # # . # # #

  ```
  The arena is four crypt chambers in the corners, joined to a central nave. The chamber walls
  (`#` inside the arena) are ossuary walls 2 m high. They make one-cell doorways, the tightest choke
  points of the campaign, so a player can hold a door. Bone piles (`c`) are destructible cover and
  burst into gems.
- **Waves.** 1: 60 R, 40 bonewalker. 2: 40 R, 30 B, 30 bonewalker. 3: 30 skitter, 70 bonewalker.
  4: 20 R, 20 L, 20 skitter, 40 bonewalker. 5: the boss, 50 bonewalker, 30 R, 20 B.
- **New monster: the bonewalker.** A tall, gaunt skeleton in a rotted shroud with a notched blade
  fused to its right forearm. It walks slowly with a stiff, hunched lurch (0.8x runner speed). Its
  attack is a backhand slash with a 0.5 s wind-up: the blade arm swings far back. It falls apart
  when it dies and leaves a bone pile (the "melt" of this level). One in five gets up once, 4 s
  later, unless the pile is shot.
- **Boss: Ossivar, the Hollow Abbot.** A 4.5 m skeletal abbot in a heavy black robe, with a crown of
  antler-like bone, a ribcage open and lit gold from inside, and a bone crozier.
  - *Crozier slam*, the close attack: he raises the staff for 0.7 s and drives it into the floor. A
    ring of bone spikes erupts around him at 3 m.
  - *Bone lances*: the gold in his chest flares for 1.0 s, then he fires 5 slow bone spears in a fan
    that you can dodge.
  - *Raise the dead*: he kneels with his arms out for 1.5 s, and every bone pile in the arena stands
    up as a bonewalker.
  - Phases: above 60% health he slams and fires lances. From 60% he raises the dead every 15 s. Below
    25% the lances come in two fans and his robe trails embers.
  - Clips: walk (a slow glide with the robe swaying), attack (the slam), special (chest flare and
    lances), summon (the kneel).
- **New weapon: the stake repeater.** A crossbow-like repeater that fires iron stakes. Every stake
  pierces 3 enemies, and the last one is pinned (stopped for 1 s). Legendary perk: pinned enemies
  are nailed together, so damage on one is shared with everything pinned near it. Mines join the
  pool.
- **Hatch: crypt stair.** A carved sarcophagus lid on a stepped stone plinth. It grinds aside, and a
  spiral stair goes down into candlelight.

### Level 4. The Foundry (molten, iron)

The crypt gives onto an old forge sunk into the rock: iron walkways, black slag, crucibles and molten
metal running in channels across the floor.

- **Palette and light.** Black iron and soot lit from below by orange-white molten metal. This is

  strips and baked orange omnis along them). A dim smoky blue comes from vents high up. Heat haze
  rises over the channels (the effect the yard already has). The grade is `foundry.cube`: orange
  highlights and steel-blue shadow.

  Molten channels (`~`) run north to south on both sides, with two short ones across the north and

  is a tactic. Crucible blocks (`oo`, 2 x 2 cells) are heavy cover in the corners. Slag carts are the
  barrels: they burst into a molten splash.
- **Waves.** 1: 60 R, 40 slagborn. 2: 40 B, 60 slagborn. 3: 30 bonewalker, 30 skitter, 40 slagborn.
  4: 20 L, 30 R, 50 slagborn. 5: the boss, 50 slagborn, 30 B, 20 bonewalker.
- **New monster: the slagborn.** A hunched furnace-man of cooled black slag over a cracked body that
  glows orange. Its head is a heavy molten-lit slot. It has broad shoulders and long arms that
  hang to the ground. It walks slowly (0.7x runner speed). It is the first basic enemy with a ranged
  attack: it stops and cups its hands for 0.7 s (the glow in them brightens), then lobs a slag
  blob in an arc. The blob leaves a burning puddle for 3 s. It also punches if you come close.
- **Boss: Anvilgrave, the Foundry Lord.** A 5 m armoured smith-demon in a riveted iron cuirass, with
  a horned furnace-mouth helm, chains on his forearms and a forge hammer the size of a door.
  - *Hammer fall*, the close attack: he lifts the hammer over his head for 1.0 s and brings it down.
    A shockwave ring spreads 8 m along the floor; jump it with the dash timing, or be outside it.
  - *Pour*: he hooks a crucible on his chain and tips it for 1.2 s, and a molten river runs 12 m in
    the direction he faces. It lasts 8 s.
  - *Bellows*: he roars, the vents flare, and 6 slagborn rise from the channels.
  - Phases: above 50% health he swings and pours. Below 50% his armour sheds and the hammer burns,
    each fall leaves a fire line, and his walk is faster. Below 20% the hammer falls come in three
    in a row.
  - Clips: walk (a heavy stomp), attack (the overhead hammer), special (the tip-pour), summon (roar,
    arms wide).
- **New weapon: the slag mortar.** A squat iron mortar that lobs shells over cover. Each shell bursts
  in a 3 m blast and leaves a molten puddle for 3 s. Legendary perk: three shells in a cluster.
  The flamer joins the pool.
- **Hatch: crucible gate.** Four iron blast doors on a slag-crusted plinth under a riveted collar. They
  slide out under the collar, and a stair goes down past a glowing pour.

  The arena is an irregular cave: the rock (`#`) bulges into the arena, and giant fungus trunks
  (`O`) stand in the corners. Spore pools (`~`) are curved, and wading one slows you. Mushroom
  clusters (`c`) are soft cover that bursts when shot. Healing caps grow at the `+` spots: a cap
  heals 25 hp and regrows in 40 s, which is the level's mechanic.
- **Waves.** 1: 60 skitter, 40 sporeling. 2: 40 R, 60 sporeling. 3: 30 B, 30 slagborn, 40 sporeling.
  4: 40 skitter, 20 L, 40 sporeling. 5: 30 bonewalker, 30 R, 40 sporeling (a dense wave).
  6: the boss, 50 sporeling, 30 skitter, 20 B.
- **New monster: the sporeling.** A waddling fungus creature about 1.2 m tall: a fat stalk body on two
  stubby root legs, a broad spotted cap glowing cyan underneath, and thin root arms. It waddles with
  a lot of sway (0.9x runner speed). Its attack is a headbutt: the cap tilts back for 0.4 s, then
  slams forward. When it dies it bursts into a spore cloud that slows anything in it for 3 s. That
  is the level's "melt": at the boss's death every sporeling bursts, a field of drifting light.
- **Boss: Mother Mycel.** A 6 m bloated fungal mass: a great bulb body crowned with shelves of
  glowing bracket fungus, ringed with thick root tentacles, and with a vertical maw in the bulb lined
  with pale gills. She moves slowly on the tentacles, like a slow tide.
  - *Root lash*, the close attack: two tentacles rear for 0.8 s and whip the ground in front of her.
  - *Spore mortar*: the bracket fungus on her crown swells and glows for 1.2 s, then fires 6 spore
    balls that land where players stand. They mark the floor 1 s before they land, so they can be
    dodged.
  - *Root eruption*: she sinks a little for 1.0 s and the floor ripples. Roots burst up in a line
    toward the farthest player.
  - Phases: above 60% health she lashes and fires mortars. From 60% the root eruptions start and
    sporelings spawn from her maw. Below 25% she uproots and chases, faster.
  - Clips: walk (a tentacle crawl), attack (the lash), special (the crown swell), summon (the maw
    opens).
- **New weapon: the seeker swarm.** A drum launcher with eight small rockets. Each rocket homes on the
  nearest enemy in a 60-degree cone. Legendary perk: a rocket that kills splits into two new
  seekers. Explosive rounds join the pool.
- **Hatch: root pit.** A knot of grey roots on a mossy mound. It unclenches outward like a flower,
  and a slope goes down into cyan light.

### Level 6. The Sunken Cathedral (gothic, stained glass)

Below the grotto is a buried cathedral: a nave of black stone, clustered columns, pews heaped into
barricades, and huge stained-glass windows lit red by something burning beyond them.

- **Palette and light.** Black and grey stone, with deep red and violet light falling through the
  stained glass in long coloured shafts (the yard's light shaft effect). Candelabra stand in pools of
  pale gold. This is the most gothic and theatrical lighting in the campaign. The fog is red-black.
  The grade is `cathedral.cube`: deep crimson with a cold lift in the shadows.
- **Layout.**
  ```

  # . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . #
  # . o . . o . . . + . . . o . . o . #

  # . c c c . . . . . . . . . c c c . #
  # . . . . . . o . . . o . . . . . . #
  g . o . . o . . . . . . . o . . o . g
  # . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . #
  # . c c c . . . . . . . . . c c c . #
  # . . . . . . o . H . o . . . . . . #
  # . c c c . . . . . . . . . c c c . #
  # . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . #
  g . o . . o . . . . . . . o . . o . g
  # . . . . . . o . . . o . . . . . . #
  # . c c c . . . . . . . . . c c c . #
  # . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . #
  # . o . . o . . . + . . . o . . o . #
  # . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . #
  # # # # # g # # # g # # # g # # # # #
  ```
  A nave runs north to south. Two rows of clustered columns (`o`) stand at 12 m and 24 m from the
  centre line. Heaped pews (`ccc`) are long barricades: they are destructible, block bullets, and
  make lanes between the columns. The hatch sits at the crossing, under the altar. Two shrines
  stand at the `+` spots north and south, which is the mechanic: touching a lit shrine gives the team
  20 s of one blessing (damage, speed or regeneration, shown by its colour), and it relights after 60 s.
- **Waves.** 1: 60 bonewalker, 40 penitent. 2: 40 R, 60 penitent. 3: 30 slagborn, 30 sporeling,
  40 penitent. 4: 30 B, 30 skitter, 40 penitent. 5: 20 L, 30 bonewalker, 50 penitent.
  6: the boss, 50 penitent, 30 bonewalker, 20 B.
- **New monster: the penitent.** A hooded wraith in a long ragged robe of matte black wool. Inside
  the hood there is only a pale mask and two cold points of light. It has no legs: the robe trails
  into tatters and it glides just above the floor, swaying (1.1x runner speed). Two long grey
  hands come out of the sleeves. Its attack is a lunge: it draws back and rises for 0.5 s, then
  rushes 4 m with its hands out. Shots pass through it while it is mid-lunge, so the dodge is the
  answer. At the boss's death every penitent unravels into ash.
- **Boss: Seraphel, the Choir Bishop.** A 5.5 m fallen bishop-demon in tall black vestments and a
  cracked gilded mitre. It has two great ragged wings of charred feathers, a face like a porcelain
  mask with the mouth sewn open, and a censer on a chain.
  - *Censer swing*, the close attack: the censer whirls for 0.7 s, then sweeps a full circle at 4 m.
  - *Choir beam*: it spreads its wings and its mouth glows for 1.5 s, then it sweeps a red beam 90
    degrees across the nave. Columns and pews block the beam, so take cover.
  - *Shroud step*: it folds its wings around itself for 0.6 s and reappears behind the player farthest
    from the others.
  - Phases: above 50% health it swings and beams. Below 50% the shroud step starts and penitents
    pour from the windows. Below 20% the beam sweeps both ways.
  - Clips: walk (a slow processional glide), attack (the censer swing), special (wings out, beam),
    summon (the wing fold).
- **New weapon: the saint's wheel.** A thrown bladed disc that bounces off walls, columns and pews up
  to 5 times and comes back to the thrower. Legendary perk: two wheels. The buzzsaws join the pool.
- **Hatch: altar seal.** A round floor seal under a stone altar, carved like a rose window. It splits
  into four stone petals that tilt down into a red-lit stair.

### Level 7. The Obsidian Rift (cold fire, void)

Under the cathedral the world comes apart: a plateau of black glass over a bottomless void, broken
basalt pillars, floating shards, and cold blue-white fire in braziers of bone.

- **Palette and light.** Glossy black obsidian (the only really shiny floor in the campaign, which is
  volcanic glass) with violet-blue rim light from the void below and cold blue fire. Pale ash falls
  (particles). The light is harsh and cold, and the warmth is gone. The grade is `rift.cube`: cold
  cyan highlights, crushed blacks and a violet cast.
- **Layout.**

  The plateau is an irregular octagon of black glass over the void (`.` outside the rim is the void,
  drawn as a deep dark gradient and fog; the arena edge `#` is basalt). Crumbling tiles (`~`) are the
  mechanic: from wave 3 on, a tile under a standing enemy or player cracks (it glows for 1.5 s) and
  falls. A player on it is put back at the nearest solid ground with 15 damage, and an enemy is
  lost. The floor slowly shrinks toward the middle. Basalt pillars (`o`) are cover, and shard
  clusters (`c`) are crystal cover that shatters.
- **Waves.** 1: 60 penitent, 40 gargoyle. 2: 40 skitter, 60 gargoyle. 3: 30 slagborn, 30 bonewalker,
  40 gargoyle. 4: 30 sporeling, 30 B, 40 gargoyle. 5: 40 R, 20 L, 40 gargoyle.
  6: the boss, 50 gargoyle, 30 penitent, 20 slagborn.
- **New monster: the gargoyle.** A crouched stone imp the size of a child, with a horned head, folded
  bat wings of cracked stone, long clawed arms and digitigrade legs. It moves in hops: it crouches,
  springs, glides 3 to 5 m on half-open wings, and lands. Each hop can clear low cover and crumbling
  gaps. Its attack is a dive from a hop: its wings flare wide for 0.4 s at the top of the hop (the
  tell), then it drops onto the target. At the boss's death every gargoyle turns to stone and
  crumbles.
- **Boss: Khar-Vaal, the Shattered Warden.** A 6 m obsidian colossus: a broad body of black glass
  plates with violet void-light in the cracks, a crown of floating shards, one arm a huge crystal
  blade and the other a fist of basalt.
  - *Blade cleave*, the close attack: he raises the blade across his body for 0.8 s and cleaves a 120
    degree arc.
  - *Shard nova*: his floating crown spins up and glows for 1.2 s, then fires a ring of 16 shards.
    The ring has two gaps, so look for them.
  - *Fracture*: he stamps, and the crumbling tiles in a ring around him fall at once, after a 1.5 s
    glow.
  - Phases: above 50% health he cleaves and fires novas. Below 50% he splits his crown into three
    orbiting shard clusters that fire on their own. Below 20% the novas come every 4 s.
  - Clips: walk (a heavy grinding stride), attack (the cleave), special (the crown spin), summon
    (the stamp).
- **New weapon: the frost lance.** A long cryo projector. It fires a piercing lance of cold that slows
  everything it passes through by 50% for 2 s, and freezes what was already slowed. Frozen
  enemies shatter when killed and damage their neighbours. Legendary perk: frozen enemies
  shatter at 25% health. Every pool weapon is now in play.
- **Hatch: void rift.** A ring of standing obsidian shards around a circle of black glass. The shards
  draw back and down into the floor, and the glass dissolves into a violet-lit vortex stair.

### Level 8. The Core (the burning heart)

The bottom is a cavern of fire around a vast throne of fused bone and iron. Rivers of lava, chained
cages, brimstone vents, and a sky of red smoke where the cave roof should be.

- **Palette and light.** Red, black and orange-white. The lava is the key light (baked emissive
  plus baked omnis), and there is a sullen red sky light. Brimstone vents puff smoke and flame
  (the fire effects). Everything is darker at the edges, and the throne at the north is the
  brightest silhouette. The grade is `core.cube`: deep red with gold highlights and a hint of
  green in the shadows for a sickly look.
- **Layout.**

  Lava (`~`) runs in short broken channels, and four lava pools sit on the diagonals round the hatch. Brimstone vents (`o`) burst on a 6 s cycle: a 1 s glow, then a
  column of fire, which is the mechanic. The throne (`T`) fills the north wall. Caged cairns (`c`)
  are cover.
- **Waves.** Every kind comes back here. 1: 60 R, 40 hellhound. 2: 30 gargoyle, 30 penitent,
  40 hellhound. 3: 30 slagborn, 30 skitter, 40 hellhound. 4: 30 bonewalker, 30 sporeling,
  40 hellhound. 5: 20 B, 20 L, 60 hellhound (the hardest regular wave of the game). 6: the boss
  with all kinds.
- **New monster: the hellhound.** A lean, long-legged demon dog with a ridge of horn spines down its
  back, a skull-like muzzle split by a glowing throat, and a burning tail. It is the fastest thing
  in the game (1.5x runner speed). It runs in a loping gallop and circles before it commits. Its
  attack is a pounce: it stops, crouches and its throat flares for 0.35 s, then it leaps 4 m. Packs
  of hellhounds flank: the director sends them in from two gaps at once.
- **Boss: the Unmaker.** The final demon is a 7 m horned colossus seated on, then rising from, the
  throne. It has a cracked crown of horns, a burning ribcage, four arms (two holding a great split
  blade, two empty and clawed), wings of smoke and chains, and hooves.
  - *Riven blade*, the close attack: it raises the split blade for 1.0 s and the blade's halves part
    and glow. Then it brings it down along a line 14 m long.
  - *Hellstorm*: all four arms rise and its ribcage blazes for 1.5 s, and fire falls on marked
    circles across the arena.
  - *Legion*: it roars, and every gap spawns the kinds of earlier levels, with one of each earlier
    boss's elite (a mini version with half the health) in phase 3.
  - Phases. Phase 1 (100 to 66%): it fights from the throne, with the blade line and the hellstorm.
    Phase 2 (66 to 33%): it rises, walks the arena, and the lava channels flood (the pools round the
    hatch spread). Phase 3 (under 33%): it calls the legion, its crown burns, and every attack comes
    faster. When it dies, the arena cools to black stone in 3 s and the last hatch opens to a shaft
    of daylight. That ends the run.
  - Clips: walk (a striding stomp, wings dragging), attack (the blade raise and fall), special (four
    arms up, the hellstorm), summon (the roar, head back).
- **New weapon: the hellfire lance.** A continuous beam that ramps up: it starts thin, grows thicker
  and hotter the longer it touches one target, and sets fire to anything it crosses. Legendary
  perk: the beam forks to a second target. Every gun is in the pool, and boss caches here drop
  Epic or better 40% of the time.
- **Hatch: the heart seal.** A black stone and iron seal shaped like a clenched heart, on a dais
  between the lava pools. Its iron bands burst open, and daylight pours up out of the shaft.

## 4. Why this shape (design notes)

- **One new thing per floor.** Each floor adds exactly one new basic monster, one boss, one gun and
  one arena mechanic. Older kinds come back in mixed waves, so the new one is learnt against
  familiar ones. Hazard mechanics affect enemies too, so they are tools and not only traps.
- **Telegraphs scale with danger.** Basic attacks wind up for 0.35 to 0.7 s, boss close attacks for
  0.7 to 1.0 s, and boss arena attacks for 1.2 to 1.5 s. Every tell is a pose change big enough to
  read in a crowd at the shared camera's distance (arms high, crouch, wings flare) plus a glow, so
  it can be seen as well as felt (no camera shake, ever; heavy hits use rumble).
- **Bosses teach in phases.** Every boss shows its close attack and one arena attack first, then adds
  a third pattern, then speeds up at the end. Each phase change is visible: the shell cracks, the
  armour sheds, the boss rises from the throne.
- **Each floor reads as deeper.** The light source changes on every floor: the sun, a broken grating,
  candles, molten metal, bioluminescence, stained glass, void-light, then lava. The cold-to-warm
  and black-to-red progression and the hatch types (lift, sluice, crypt stair, crucible, root pit,
  altar, rift, heart) tell the story of going down without any text.
- **Loot is a showpiece.** Rarity is a colour seen from across the arena (glow, ring, beam, HUD
  tag), and drops are power-ups at a better grade, so there is still no inventory.

## 5. Asset contract (what the code reads)

Level ids: `yard`, `sluice`, `ossuary`, `foundry`, `grotto`, `cathedral`, `rift`, `core`.

For each level `<id>` other than the yard (which keeps what it has, plus a hatch):

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `assets/levels/<id>.cwlevel` | Layout, props, gameplay markers, bake-only lights, roster. A line format (below), generated by `tools/gen-levels.mjs`. |
| `assets/scenes/<id>.cwscene` | The level's sky or ambient, key and rim lights, environment (fog), post (grade LUT, bloom, exposure). The same node names as `arena.cwscene`, so code that reads the yard's `Sun`, `Rim`, `Env` and `Post` reads these. |
| `assets/grade/<id>.cube` | The level's colour grade (`tools/gen-level-luts.mjs`). |
| `assets/textures/<id>_floor.png` | The floor's albedo, 1024 square, tiling (`tools/gen-level-textures.mjs`). Its relief is painted in, because the floor draws baked and unlit, as the yard's does. |
| `assets/models/<id>_*.cwchar` | The props, the hatch, the new basic monster and the boss (section 6). |
| `assets/lightmaps/<id>.cwlm` | The level's bake, made from the level as `tools/levelview/levelbuild.c` builds it (the levelview cart's `LEVEL_BAKE`, or the game's `HORDE_BAKE` once it builds levels with the same file). |

Monster and boss `.cwchar` clips, in the order the game indexes them:

| Clip | Basic monster | Boss |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | walk, 6 frames, a loop | walk, 10 frames, a loop |
| 1 | attack, 3 frames: wind-up (the tell, held), strike, recovery | the close attack, 3 frames: wind-up, strike, recovery |
| 2 | - | special (the arena attack), 3 frames: tell (held), release, recovery |
| 3 | - | summon or roar, 2 frames: gather (held), release |

The level's static scenery (the floor, the props, the hazard strips, the lights) is built by
`tools/levelview/levelbuild.c` (`levelbuild_parse`, `levelbuild_build`, `levelbuild_hatch`,
`levelbuild_lightmap`), which the game links. Everything goes under one `Level` node, with the level's
lighting scene instanced under it as `Light`. The bake matches meshes by node path under that node and
by geometry, so a bake made by the levelview cart applies in the game unchanged. While a level other
than the yard is up, the game hides the yard's own `Sun`, `Rim`, `Env` and `Post`, because the level brings
its own.

The hatch `.cwchar`: clip 0 is its opening, 6 frames from shut (frame 0) to open (frame 5). It is
rigid, one instance at the level's `door` position. Group 1 is its glow (the runes or the light
below), which the code can tint and fade up as it opens. The closed hatch is flush enough to walk
over (under 0.5 m, with sloped edges), so the team can spawn standing on it.

Weapon pickups (`assets/models/weapon_<name>.cwchar`, one frame, group 1 the glow) and the loot cache
(`assets/models/loot_cache.cwchar`, clip 0 shut and opening in 4 frames, group 1 the glow trim the code
tints with the rarity colour).

### The `.cwlevel` format

Plain text, one record a line, `#` starts a comment, numbers in metres and radians, world axes as
the game uses them (x east, z south, y up; the sim's y is world z). Records:

```
level <id> <name...>                 the id and the display name
waves <n>                            waves on this level, the last one the boss wave
roster <kind> ...                    the kinds this level can spawn (runner brute bloater skitter ...)
wave <i> <kind>:<share> ...          the shares of wave i (1-based); "boss" marks the boss wave
monster <file>                       the level's new basic monster
boss <file> <name...>                the level's boss and its display name
scene <file>                         the lighting scene
lightmap <file>                      the bake
floor <albedo> <tile_m> <rough> <tint r g b>             the floor material: texture tile size in metres
void <r>                             the arena is an octagonal plateau r metres out (the rift): the floor
                                     beyond it falls dark
prop <name> <file> <tx> <rep> <rough> <metal> <solid>    a prop model: name, file, the view's surface kind
                                     (pillar, armor, hide, ground), texture repeat, roughness, metalness;
                                     solid: what each placement is to the sim. 0 scenery (no obstacle),
                                     1 an indestructible obstacle (a pier, a column), 2 a wall that falls
                                     to blasts, 3 a crate (bursts into gems), 4 a barrel (explodes)
at <name> <x> <y> <z> <yaw> [<hx> <hz>]   a placement of prop <name> (y: the base height; a placement on top of
                                     another prop has that prop's top as y). hx hz: its obstacle half
                                     extents along world x and z (rotated bounds), present when solid
obstacle <kind> <file>               the model the sim's crates, barrels, walls or pillars draw with on
                                     this level (kind: crate barrel wall pillar)
door <file> <x> <z>                  the hatch (always 0 0)
spawn <x> <z> <hw>                   a gap the horde comes in through, its half width
pickup <x> <z>                       a power-up spot
hazard <kind> <x> <z> <hx> <hz>      an axis-aligned hazard box (water, molten, spore, crumble, lava, vent)
shrine <x> <z>                       a shrine (cathedral) or healing cap (grotto)
light <type> <x> <y> <z> <tx> <ty> <tz> <r> <g> <b> <energy> <range> <angle> <penumbra> <bake_only>
                                     a light: spot (aimed at tx ty tz) or omni (target ignored);
                                     bake_only 1: in the lightmap only, as the yard's floodlights
```

## 6. Where everything comes from

- `tools/blender/levelchars.py`: the new monsters and bosses, rigged and baked to `.cwchar` with the
  same library as the yard's crowd (`cwchar_lib.py`).
- `tools/blender/levelprops.py`: each level's props, the hatches, the weapon pickups and the loot cache.
- `tools/gen-levels.mjs`: the `.cwlevel` layouts from the design above (seeded, deterministic).
- `tools/check-levels.mjs`: the scenery checks. No floating or sunken props (every base on the floor
  or on the prop under it, within 3 cm), no overhang, nothing inside the hatch's clear circle, solids
  that don't overlap. `--control` lifts one prop 0.5 m, and the check must fail on it.
- `tools/gen-level-textures.mjs`, `tools/gen-level-luts.mjs`: the floor textures (and the melt texture the molten
  and lava strips draw with, `textures/lava_floor.png`) and the grades.
- `tools/levelview/`: `levelbuild.c`, the level builder the game links, and the `hordeshooter_levelview` cart. It
  shows each level with its crowd or its boss going through its clips and its hatch opening (left and right
  change the level, up and down the camera, fire what stands on the floor, the blast button the hatch). Natively,
  `--bake <dir> [--level <id>]` bakes the lightmaps.
- Contact sheets: `levelchars.py` and `levelprops.py` take `--sheet <dir>` and render every clip frame (the game's
  camera angle and a side view) headless, for review before a level is in a cart.
